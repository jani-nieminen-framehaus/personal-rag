# Daily rituals for solo photo business

The single biggest lever for a solo photographer isn't gear — it's the
five-minute morning ritual.

## Before the day starts

- Review the day's shoots and travel time on a single map view.
- Check the weather (golden hour timing shifts with cloud cover).
- Pick the two outcomes that, if they happen, make the day a win. Write
  them down.
- Empty the camera bag into the laundry basket. Refill it from the freshly
  cleaned kit. This is the cheapest insurance you have.

## After the last shoot

- Offload cards to two destinations before you sleep. The card is a single
  point of failure until you do.
- Skim the first 5 frames of each shoot for focus and exposure problems.
  Catching a missed-focus batch in 2 minutes beats discovering it the next
  morning.
- Send a "sneak peek" to the client the same day. It costs nothing and
  buys goodwill at a 10:1 ratio.

## Once a week

- Backup audit: are the offloads mirrored to the second location? Pick a
  random day and verify.
- Inbox zero on the client pipeline. Every prospect should have a next
  action with a date.
- One piece of content — a frame, a behind-the-scenes, a tip. Friday
  morning is fine.
